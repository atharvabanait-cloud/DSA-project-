Priority queue (emergency) vs FIFO queue (normal)

Normal traffic → FIFO queue → first in, first out.
Emergency traffic → priority queue → lowest priority number leaves first, ties go by arrival.

Road network as a graph (adjacency list, intersection = node, road = edge)

Format: intersections (id, x, y, signal) and roads (from, to, lanes, speedLimit km/h). The importer validates it and uses the first road's speed limit. The engine simulates one signalised four-way junction; the other nodes appear in the graph and BFS only. CSV is not implemented.

Why a queue?

Vehicles at a red light leave in the order they arrived. That is FIFO, so a queue models it directly. Here the front vehicle is released only by dequeue(), so removing the queue would stop the simulation from releasing vehicles.

Traffic management rules

Fixed: green stays as set. Adaptive: green = 6 s + 1.5 s per waiting vehicle on that axis, limited to 6–45 s. So a queue over 10 gets a longer green and a queue under 3 gets a short one.
Emergency: when an emergency vehicle is in the priority queue its axis gets green (after yellow and all-red), is held until it crosses, then normal cycling resumes.

Presentation demo

  1. Load "Normal traffic" and press Start.
  2. Watch the signal go red; vehicles enqueue V01 → V02 → V03 in the FIFO panel.
  3. The front vehicle is highlighted (FRONT and REAR shown).
  4. On green, the log shows PEEK and DEQUEUE one vehicle at a time.
  5. FIFO status stays PASSED. On the DSA page, break FIFO on purpose to see FAILED.
  6. Spawn an emergency vehicle: the priority queue panel fills and its axis gets green.
  7. Switch to Adaptive mode and open the charts.
